The Greatest Giver Of All…God

December 19, 2023 2 min read Love & Lordship

Merry CHRISTmas and Happy New Year in CHRIST!

As we close 2023 in this wonderful CHRISTmas season and enter into the New Year, I pray that God has richly blessed you and yours…and that you are living in such a way that His blessings, and even more so, His Presence is filling you and overflowing into others.

Give, and it will be given to you. They will pour into your lap a good measure—pressed down, shaken together, and running over. For by your standard of measure it will be measured to you in return. Luke 6:38

Each one must do just as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver. 2 Corinthians 9:7

It is more blessed to give than to receive. Acts 20:35

Thanksgiving, giving and blessing are intertwined in God’s Kingdom and design!

Thanks be to God for His indescribable gift! 2 Corinthians 9:15

Are you looking forward in faith to all God has in store for you and doing so with gratitude, peace, joy and love found only in His Indescribable Gift, Jesus? Christ makes you new (1 Corinthians 5:17) so that God can do something new in and through you!

Behold, I am going to do something new, Now it will spring up; Will you not be aware of it? I will even make a roadway in the wilderness, Rivers in the desert. Isaiah 43:19

Thanks for your prayers and generosity! As The Lord and His Spirit lead, would you prayerfully consider a financial gift to Love & Lord allowing Him to reach more through this ministry and message for His Kingdom and Glory?

Love and Lordship…Food for Thought – If God is not leading you to give to Love & Lordship, please continue praying and asking Him to show you who He would have you partner with and then be obedient and faithful out of love for Him. He will bless you as you are a cheerful giver!

Love and Lordship…Action Item(s) –

  1. Please join us as prayer warrior for Love & Lordship as The Lord leads you. If you choose to do so please let me know at loveandlordship@gmail.com.
  2. Prayerfully consider a one-time or ongoing financial gift to Love & Lordship
  3. If not this ministry keep praying until The Lord shows you where He would have you partner in ministry for His Kingdom and Glory.

Love and Lordship Prayer Focus

Heavenly Father, thank You for giving us the greatest gift ever given in Christ coming to earth, living a perfect life and dying in my place. I can never outgive You but I desire to give as I can and as You lead as a reflection of my love for You in all that You’ve given me.  In Jesus Precious Name I pray.  Amen.
